The 1:30 Team Pitch

Siya — Intro + The Problem

"Good morning, judges. I come from an area where a local river became severely contaminated by acidic water from a mine, and the community found out far too late. Manual sampling leaves dangerous blind spots between when water quality changes and when someone notices. That delay is the problem we decided to solve. Enter AquaSentinel."

Karabo — The Solution + Technology

"AquaSentinel is an AI-powered IoT water monitoring and compliance platform. Our device uses an ESP32 connected to sensors tracking pH, turbidity, temperature, and conductivity. Placed strategically upstream and downstream, our AI continuously compares readings against historical baselines. If conditions suddenly shift, the system instantly detects the anomaly—identifying what changed, where, and when—generating an automated incident record."

Unathi — Compliance + Accountability

Amu — Impact + Close
