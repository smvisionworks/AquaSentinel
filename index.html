Our Solutions

Four Pillars, One Automated Loop

AquaSentinel is built around a simple idea: catch contamination at the source, respond to it physically and immediately, and keep an evidence trail that stands up to scrutiny — without a person having to be watching.

Monitor

A water sensor and a gas sensor per node — reading Conductivity (as a turbidity proxy), Water Level, Water Flow, and Gas (CO₂) — continuously sample every monitored site.
